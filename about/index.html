About the app

Prepare repeatable SVG batches for your laser

LaserBatcher helps laser makers turn one SVG design into a sheet of copies, inspect operation assignments, and keep job information with the exported file.

Created by Off the Wall Creations, it runs in your browser. Open a design, choose your sheet settings, review the mapping, then render and download an SVG for your laser software.

Open LaserBatcher

From one design to a prepared sheet

Set sheet width and height, horizontal and vertical margins, spacing, and copy count. LaserBatcher repeats the source canvas in a regular arrangement; Fill selects the capacity for those settings. Use the main canvas to inspect the source or rendered batch, zoom, pan, and change the preview background.

Assign Engrave, Score, Interior Cut, Exterior Cut, or Ignore to elements and groups. Choose operation order within each copy or across the sheet. Enhanced thumbnails make fine strokes easier to inspect without changing exported styling.

Keep useful job information with the SVG

Add a part name, order number, batch, and yield. Estimated Runtime is an optional duration you enter for the entire output job. Re-open a supported LaserBatcher export in Edit metadata to update these fields without laying out the artwork again.

Local artwork processing

SVG analysis, layout, preview generation and metadata editing happen locally in your browser. LaserBatcher does not upload your SVG artwork for processing. Downloads are separate files; your uploaded original is unchanged.

The site uses Google Analytics for traffic reporting, and the workspace includes an optional Buy me a coffee support widget. These external services can make network requests; local SVG processing does not mean the entire site is network-free. Once the workspace has installed its offline cache, it can also reopen without a connection.

Know the boundaries

LaserBatcher does not automatically nest shapes, edit geometry, infer materials, estimate runtime, choose power settings, or control a laser. It preserves supported machine settings already in a file. Not every SVG feature is supported for batching; review Issues before export.

SVG document order and any regenerated native settings do not guarantee execution order in another application. Check dimensions, operation order, power, speed and passes in your laser software before running a job.
